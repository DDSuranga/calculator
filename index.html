VAT / Discount Calculator

Add or remove VAT (Value Added Tax) or discounts from prices. Perfect for shoppers, business owners, and accountants.

About the VAT Calculator

This VAT calculator helps you add tax to a net price or remove VAT from a tax-inclusive price.

How to use it

Enter the amount, type the VAT rate, then choose Add VAT or Remove VAT.

Formula

Add VAT: Total = Amount x (1 + Rate / 100). Remove VAT: Net = Total / (1 + Rate / 100).

Example

For Rs. 10,000 at 18 percent VAT, the VAT-inclusive total is Rs. 11,800.

Can I use this for sales tax?

Yes. It works for any percentage-based tax similar to VAT.

What does Remove VAT mean?

It calculates the original net price from a price that already includes VAT.

Can the VAT rate be changed?

Yes. Enter the tax rate that applies to your country or invoice.

Loan EMI Calculator

Calculate monthly loan payments, total payment, and total interest for personal, vehicle, housing, or business loans.

Enter loan details and calculate your monthly EMI.

About the Loan EMI Calculator

This calculator estimates the fixed monthly payment for a loan and shows total payment and total interest.

How to use it

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, loan term, and choose whether the term is in years or months.

Formula

EMI = P x R x (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N - 1). For 0 percent interest, EMI = P / N.

Example

A Rs. 500,000 loan for 5 years at 10 percent interest gives an estimated monthly EMI.

What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Installment, the fixed amount paid each month.

Does this include fees?

No. It calculates loan payment from principal, rate, and term only.

Can I calculate a 0 percent loan?

Yes. The calculator divides the loan amount by the number of months.

BMI Calculator

About the BMI Calculator

This BMI calculator estimates body mass index from weight and height, then shows the common BMI category.

How to use it

Enter your weight and height, choose the correct units, and press Calculate.

Formula

BMI = weight in kilograms / (height in meters x height in meters).

Example

A person who weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 m tall has a BMI of about 22.9.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening number and does not replace medical advice.

Can I use pounds and feet?

Yes. The calculator converts imperial units before calculating BMI.

What is a normal BMI range?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly considered the normal range for adults.

Profit Margin Calculator

Calculate profit amount, profit margin, and markup percentage from cost price and selling price.

Enter cost and selling price to calculate profit margin.

About the Profit Margin Calculator

This calculator shows profit amount, profit margin, and markup from cost price and selling price.

How to use it

Enter the cost price and selling price, then press Calculate.

Formula

Profit = Selling price - Cost price. Margin = Profit / Selling price x 100. Markup = Profit / Cost price x 100.

Example

If cost is Rs. 100 and selling price is Rs. 150, profit is Rs. 50, margin is 33.33 percent, and markup is 50 percent.

What is profit margin?

Profit margin shows profit as a percentage of selling price.

What is markup?

Markup shows profit as a percentage of cost price.

Can profit be negative?

Yes. If selling price is lower than cost price, the result shows a loss.

Break-Even Calculator

Find how many units you need to sell to cover fixed costs and calculate break-even sales value.

Enter business cost details to calculate break-even point.

About the Break-Even Calculator

This calculator estimates how many units must be sold to cover fixed and variable costs.

How to use it

Enter fixed costs, selling price per unit, and variable cost per unit.

Formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs / (Selling price per unit - Variable cost per unit).

Example

If fixed costs are Rs. 1,000, price is Rs. 50, and variable cost is Rs. 30, break-even is 50 units.

What are fixed costs?

Fixed costs are costs that do not change directly with each unit sold, such as rent or salaries.

What is variable cost?

Variable cost is the cost linked to each unit, such as materials or packaging.

Why must price be higher than variable cost?

Each sale must contribute something toward fixed costs; otherwise break-even cannot be reached.

ROI Calculator

Measure investment performance by calculating net profit and return on investment percentage.

Enter investment and final value to calculate ROI.

About the ROI Calculator

This ROI calculator measures return on investment by comparing the investment amount with the final value.

How to use it

Enter the original investment amount and the final value or return amount.

Formula

Net profit = Final value - Investment amount. ROI = Net profit / Investment amount x 100.

Example

If you invest Rs. 1,000 and the final value is Rs. 1,250, net profit is Rs. 250 and ROI is 25 percent.

What does ROI mean?

ROI means return on investment, a percentage that shows gain or loss compared with the amount invested.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. If the final value is lower than the investment, ROI is negative.

Does ROI include time?

No. This calculator shows simple ROI and does not annualize the return.

Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate simple interest and total amount from principal, annual interest rate, and time in years.

Enter principal, rate, and time to calculate simple interest.

About the Simple Interest Calculator

This calculator finds interest earned or charged using principal, annual rate, and time in years.

How to use it

Enter the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period in years.

Formula

Simple interest = Principal x Rate x Time / 100. Total amount = Principal + Interest.

Example

For Rs. 1,000 at 10 percent for 2 years, interest is Rs. 200 and total amount is Rs. 1,200.

What is simple interest?

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal amount.

Is this compound interest?

No. Compound interest adds interest to the balance over time.

Can I enter decimal years?

Yes. You can use values such as 1.5 years for one year and six months.
